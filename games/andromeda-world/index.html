THE UTOPIAN GALAXIES

Every life here begins inside a petal sac of living abalone, sealed and glowing, somewhere in the world.

Inside it, you choose who you are. When you are ready, the petals open, and the world finds you wherever you emerge.

Computer: WASD or arrows to walk, Shift to run, drag to look, E to interact.
Phone: left circle to walk, drag the screen to look, tap the button that appears.
