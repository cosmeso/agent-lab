For adults 18+. Begin as a new life inside a petal sac, choose who you are, and emerge in the 3D world of the Utopian Galaxies, played in first person (or third, with V). Story Mode, Chapter 1: The Sliven Burrow, written by Abimael: play air hopscotch with six-year-old Madison and her friends Migi, Armius, Marla and Sady, chase the trees that don't move, feel the ground move like a tsunami, sit down to dinner with Grandma and Grandpa, gather at the Remembrance's blue fire, train for two weeks, get caught by the guards, catch hush-tree leaves, dive under the moss to pack the sliven nest, and bring it down from the Crystal Cave. Live among Madison's family in a wide dirt clearing of hut homes ringed by forest, with Ruru farms, an orchard, the Sanctuary of seven pools, the forbidden oldest willow, deep caverns with smelting coffers, the Golden Wood of foxrids, and the Skyrail to Alio. Everyone plays together in one shared world.
