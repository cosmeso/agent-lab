Begin as a new life inside a petal sac, choose who you are, and emerge in the 3D world of the Utopian Galaxies, played in first person (or third, with V). Live among Madison's family in a wide dirt clearing of hut homes ringed by forest, with Ruru farms, an orchard, the Sanctuary of seven pools (healing, soul, transference, hearth, moon, mind, liver), and the forbidden oldest willow. Go down into the caverns for ore, crystals, buried chests and smelting coffers that upgrade your blades, magic, armor, healing and shoes. Take the Skyrail to Alio, walk the Golden Road into the forest of gold, and fight foxrids and Entliros with sword, machete, trident, bow and Liev, all aimed by you. Everyone plays together in one shared world.
