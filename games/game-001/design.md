**Game Name:** **Pixel Puzzle: Escape the Grid**

**Goal:** Navigate a colorful pixel through a maze of moving obstacles to reach the exit.

**Controls:** Use arrow keys to move the pixel up, down, left, and right. Press 'S' to shoot a pulse that clears a path through solid blocks.

**Rules:** The pixel starts at the top-left corner. Navigate through a grid filled with moving blocks and walls. Obstacles will move in random patterns. You cannot move through walls or solid blocks. The pixel cannot jump; it must slide and move.

**How You Win:** Reach the exit on the far side of the grid within the time limit. The game ends once you reach the exit or time runs out.

**How You Lose:** You lose if you touch a moving obstacle, get stuck in a corner, or fall off the grid. Time limit is usually around 30 seconds.
