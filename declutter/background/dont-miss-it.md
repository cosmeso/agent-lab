Use the Gmail connector (personal Gmail) and the Microsoft 365 connector (my University of Arizona Outlook email and calendar). Check email received in about the last 3.5 hours, and Outlook calendar events in the next 6 hours.

- a bill, a payment failure, an overdraft, or an unexpected charge

- a calendar event starting in the next 6 hours that I might not know about

If there IS something urgent, reply in this shape, 6 lines at most:
🚨 <count> thing(s) need you
• <who/what> · <why it matters> · <Gmail or School> · <suggested next step>

Marketing, newsletters, social notifications, receipts and shipping updates are never urgent.
